gws-cli privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

gws-cli is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail and Google Drive, through Google's APIs, with the owner's permission.
  • What it does with it: reads email, saves attachments and copies files to and from the owner's Drive, on the owner's own computers.
  • What it shares: nothing. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Where data is kept: on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google account.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
